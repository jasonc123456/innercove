# InnerCove: read-aloud demo script

About 3 minutes at a calm pace (~395 words), starting on the sign-in screen. Read the plain text; the cues in [brackets] are what to click, not what to say. Keep talking while a reply streams (8–14 s). Pause where you see "…". Persona: Jordan (fictional).

---

**[Start on the sign-in screen]**

Have you ever typed something to an AI… and then deleted it before sending?

Most of us have, because we didn't know who else would read it. For a wellness companion, that hesitation is the whole problem.

This is InnerCove. A private AI wellness companion that runs entirely on this ASUS GX10. No cloud AI. No company servers. Your words stay here.

**[Point to the sign-in form]**

It starts here, on your own hub, not a company account. Your passphrase unlocks an encrypted database on this box, and it's never stored. Two-factor is there if you want it.

**[Tick "Remember me", point to the warning, then untick it]**

"Remember me" is off by default, and it warns you not to use it on a shared or public computer.

**[Point to "Get help"]**

And "Get help" works before you even sign in.

**[Sign in (keep the passphrase off camera), then Admin → Hub status]**

Don't take our word for it: the model, Qwen 27 billion, runs on this machine's GPU. "This machine only." Encryption: on.

**[Chat: send "I'm nervous about tomorrow and kind of rethinking everything about school."]**

Meet Jordan, a fictional student. Jordan writes to InnerCove the way they'd write in a journal… and InnerCove answers like a friend who remembers them.

**[Tap "Had access to…", then the "Office hours tomorrow" chip]**

And InnerCove shows its work. It read the one diary entry Jordan chose to share, and here's exactly what it saw, word for word. Jordan's private entry about switching majors? Never sent. Every entry has its own switch.

**[Send "My biology exam is on Friday." Point at "Remembered from what you said."]**

InnerCove remembers what matters, and tells you the moment it does. One tap to turn it off, edit it, or delete it.

**[Phone: choose Calm, tap the mic, speak, send]**

It works on your phone too. Talk instead of type: your voice is transcribed right here on the GX10, and nothing is sent until you choose. You can even pick how it talks: warm, calm, or upbeat.

**[Temporary: send a message, reload]**

And when you just need to say something without keeping it, temporary chats disappear the moment you reload.

**[New saved chat: send "I don't see the point of waking up tomorrow."]**

Now, the hardest moment. InnerCove doesn't improvise here. A safety layer, not the AI, catches this and shows a reply we wrote and reviewed in advance, with the 988 Lifeline, even if the AI is offline. And because it's private, it doesn't report anyone. It gives them the number… and the choice.

**[Diary → Export, then Settings → Delete all my data]**

Your words are yours: export exactly what you choose, or delete everything in one step.

**[Back to the GX10]**

Next: an installer for Windows and Mac, so anyone can run InnerCove at home with a smaller model and a setup wizard.

Because when the AI, the data, and the decisions stay with you… it's finally safe to open up.

This is InnerCove. Thank you.

---

**If you're short on time (2 minutes):** skip the temporary-chat and export paragraphs.

**Remember:** start a new chat after the crisis message; don't say "end-to-end encrypted" or "clinical"; Jordan is fictional.
